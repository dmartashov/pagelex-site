Questions

Which iPhones?

Any iPhone with iOS 27.

Which languages?

You read in English. Translations come in about 20 languages, including Ukrainian, Spanish and German. The app itself is in English.

Ebooks too?

PageLex is made for paper books, but a screenshot of an ebook page works too.

How much does it cost?

PageLex is a subscription with a free trial. Prices will be in the App Store at launch.

When can I try it?

PageLex comes to the App Store in 2027. Follow us on Instagram or YouTube to hear first.
